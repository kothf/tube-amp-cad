Board Design: help

The board takes its parts and connections from the schematic in the Circuit CAD, which keeps the layout in the circuit file (File → Save there). New parts wait in a row below the board: drag them onto it. Thin lines (the ratsnest) show connections still to route; route them as tracks on the top or bottom copper. The design-rule check lists clearances that are too small (high-voltage nets need more), shorts, copper off the board and parts not placed yet.

Design rules

Valve circuits run at several hundred volts: nets above the high-voltage threshold (from the simulation) get the HV clearance and the power-track width. Every net belongs to a class: automatically (HV by voltage, GND is Power, the rest Signal) or as you set it below.
