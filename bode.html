How it is computed

The generator drives the circuit with 1 V at each frequency; every part is linearized at the circuit's idle operating point (all generators off), so this is the small-signal response. Tubes include their interelectrode capacitances (Miller effect); transformers their inductance and leakage. Large-signal effects (clipping, transformer saturation) show on the oscilloscope and the spectrum analyzer instead.
