Design from a description

Describe the amplifier or stage: tubes, output power, load, supply, gain, anything you care about. The AI designs it from the library's parts, the CAD draws it on a new sheet and simulates it, and if the Checks find problems they go back to the AI for a revision. It replaces the circuit on the sheet (Ctrl+Z brings it back). Uses your AI key.

Tube from a datasheet

Pick a tube's datasheet (PDF). The AI reads its ratings, heater, pinout and points along the plate curves; a Koren model is fitted to the points and shown against them. Review it in the AI tab, then add the tube to the library. The PDF goes to your AI provider; uses your AI key.

Tube Amp CAD: help

Hover any underlined label in the app for what it means. The full manual explains every part, value and instrument, with a design cookbook.

How to

  • Place: pick a part on the left (or type in the search box: a name, a part number like 2N3904 or EL84, or a value like 47k or 100n, then Enter) and click the sheet. Shift-click places several.
  • Wire: click a pin, click corners, finish on a pin or wire. W starts wires anywhere.
  • Edit: right-click a part or wire for Rotate, Mirror, Duplicate, Delete, Copy and Paste; the same are in Edit ▾.
  • Move: drag parts (wires follow) or drag a wire segment sideways. Sheet frames are locked; unlock one in its inspector to move it.
  • View: wheel zooms, Space+drag or middle-drag pans (a tap of Space rotates), F fits the sheet you are on, again for all sheets.
  • Measure: every wire shows its DC voltage and current (View ▾ turns them on and off); hover a wire for both. Wire an Oscilloscope to see waveforms; double-click it for the full scope. Windows ▾ → Spectrum analyzer shows the harmonics of its channels, Bode plotter their frequency response (gain and phase from 1 Hz to 1 MHz).
  • Ratings: with nothing selected, the inspector's Checks list every part beyond its rating; for tubes Pa, Pg2, screen voltage, cathode current, heater-to-cathode voltage (set Heater DC potential for a raised heater) and the grid circuit resistance. A tube's inspector shows each as a share of its rating.
  • Simulate: Live re-simulates after every edit. Turn it off to simulate only on ▶ Simulate (Ctrl+Enter), which always runs until the circuit has settled.
  • Switch: double-click to flip it; sections named SA1.1, SA1.2… flip together.
  • Standards: symbols follow IEC 60617 and designations use the classic letter codes (R, C, L, T, VL, VD, VT, HL, SA, BA, G, P; ГОСТ 2.710). Add a Drawing frame for an IEC 61082 sheet with reference grid and title block.
  • Several sheets: File → Add sheet… places a further sheet; carry a rail or signal to another sheet with Sheet connectors of the same name. File → Export sheet… saves one sheet or each sheet as PDF or PNG.
  • Panels: drag the edge of the palette, the inspector or the BOM to resize it; double-click the edge to reset it. Click a palette group's title to fold it.
